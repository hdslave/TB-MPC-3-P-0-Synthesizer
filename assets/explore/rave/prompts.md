## Screen directions

Use case: ui-mockup. A horizontal triptych of three distinctly different original pixel-graphic design directions for the live TB-MPC-3-P-0 bass sequencer. Each third of the image is one complete landscape 320x240 hardware LCD shown flat, edge-to-edge with no hardware housing or external captions. Each starts with a narrow black row of soft-key tabs, then a generous horizontal pitch-roll with 16 steps, gate notes, slide connectors, accents, big hex seed like A7F2, running position and pot controls in a thin footer. Old-school warehouse rave, acid-house industrial and melted smiley art. Left screen: yellow and hot pink over black plum, cut-paper typography, distorted checker flag folded like a ribbon and a big dripping skewed smiley in the title strip, Cyrillic-inspired heavy italic lettering (English labels). Middle screen: mint on deep violet, liquid offset-print grin and concentric whorls in a negative space poster, hard-edged CRT instrumentation. Right screen: orange and sulphur on carbon, oblique caution stencil and dripping radioactive smiley on a metal control ticket. Readable and organized as actual music gear. Bold flat screenprint pixel art meant for a small display, sharp ink shapes and a few strong spots of color, no photo, shadows, device frames, desktop GUI or logos. Very wide 3:1 canvas, all three screens visible separately side by side.
