## Striped orbit

Use case: original graphic asset for the acid bass instrument TB-MPC-3-P-0. Create a single bold original 1992 underground electronic record sleeve illustration with TWO flat spot colors only: solid ink BLACK shapes and a plain fluorescent yellow-green (acid citron #d8ff43) background. Flat print, perfectly front facing. Subject is an asymmetric, tilted, three-dimensional toroidal waveform, like a loop of corrugated sound folding back through itself, drawn in thick black optical contour stripes. A single broad orbit with a very clear central hole, sculptural and slightly warped, strong tilted silhouette. Make the stripes distinct; roughly 9-12 broad stripes, with some solid black shadow forms and large unprinted negative spaces; optimize for reduction to 94 x 54 pixels. Composition is horizontal, the object centered on a clean uninterrupted citron rectangular field with narrow native margins. Visually striking music-poster graphic with no text, no words, no letters, no numbers, no sparkles, no chrome, no drop, no typography, no branding, no mockup, no hardware. Fill the frame with the single object. It must look like a designer's original screen print, not a photo or generic emoji.
